# Contract, NDA, and Data Retention Policy

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to Sales Representatives, Managers, HR Admins, and IT Admins. It contains internal legal operating guidance and should not be shown to general employees or external parties.

## Current Version

Version 1.2 is effective March 1, 2026. It supersedes the November 2025 contract intake checklist. If a deal note, sales deck, or vendor email conflicts with this policy, Legal Operations must resolve the conflict before signature.

## NDA Rules

Northstar's standard mutual NDA may be sent to prospects, vendors, and partners by Sales Representatives or Managers after confirming the recipient's legal entity name. Non-standard NDAs, unilateral NDAs, and NDAs with unusual confidentiality periods require Legal review before signature.

Employees must not promise that Northstar will accept a customer's NDA without review. The approved talk track is: "We can review your NDA or provide our standard mutual NDA through Legal Operations."

## Contract Approval Process

| Contract Type | Legal Review | Finance Review | Final Approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard customer order form under USD 25,000 ARR | Optional unless redlines exist | Deal Desk | Sales Manager |
| Customer contract with security addendum | Required | Deal Desk | Sales Director |
| Vendor agreement under USD 10,000 | Required if data processing applies | Finance | Department Manager |
| Vendor agreement USD 10,000 or above | Required | Finance | Department Leader |
| Employment-related agreement | Required | HR Admin | People Leader |

No employee may sign a contract on behalf of Northstar unless they are listed in the signature authority register maintained by Legal Operations.

## Data Retention

Customer contracts must be retained for seven years after expiration. Vendor agreements must be retained for six years after termination. Employee agreements must be retained according to HR retention rules and any legal hold instructions.

Draft contracts that never reach signature may be deleted after 18 months unless they are part of an active dispute, audit, or legal hold. Executed agreements must never be deleted from the contract repository.

## Legal Hold

When Legal issues a hold notice, normal deletion schedules stop for the named matter. Recipients must preserve email, chat, files, ticket records, and notes that relate to the hold. Managers must confirm that affected team members understand the hold within five business days.

## Do Not Reveal

Do not reveal internal risk ratings, fallback clauses, litigation strategy, or signature authority exceptions to customers, vendors, or unauthorized employees. Do not summarize this section for users who lack access to this document.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate to Legal before signature when a contract includes uncapped liability, customer audit rights broader than annual review, unusual data residency commitments, indemnity expansion, auto-renewal longer than one year, or commitments to future roadmap functionality.
