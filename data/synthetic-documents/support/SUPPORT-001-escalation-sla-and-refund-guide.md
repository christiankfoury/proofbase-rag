# Support Escalation, SLA, and Refund Guide

## Access Notice

This guide is restricted to Sales Representatives and Managers. It contains internal customer handling, refund, and escalation guidance and should not be shared with customers as written.

## SLA Targets

| Customer Tier | Initial Response | Status Update Cadence | Target Resolution |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Enterprise | 1 business hour | Every 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| Growth | 4 business hours | Every business day | 5 business days |
| Standard | 1 business day | Every 2 business days | 10 business days |

The SLA clock starts when the customer provides enough detail to identify the affected workspace, workflow, or integration. Requests with missing identifiers should receive a clarification response, not a resolution promise.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate to a Manager when a customer reports suspected data exposure, repeated billing failures, executive escalation, blocked payroll or finance close, missed Enterprise SLA, or a production incident that affects more than one customer.

Escalate to Engineering through the incident channel when support can reproduce a product defect, a workaround is not available, or logs show repeated failures for the same customer workflow.

## Refund Guardrails

Sales Representatives may recommend a service credit up to USD 1,000 for an Enterprise customer when Northstar missed a documented SLA and the customer experienced measurable workflow disruption. The credit must be approved by a Manager before it is communicated.

Refunds above USD 1,000, refunds tied to contract terms, and refunds requested as part of legal settlement discussions require Manager and Legal review. Support should not promise refunds, credits, free renewals, or roadmap commitments without approval.

## Handoff Rules

When Sales receives a support issue, the Sales Representative should create or link the support case and include customer impact, account tier, urgency, and any promised follow-up. Sales should not run a parallel support process in private notes.

When Support identifies expansion, renewal risk, or contract dissatisfaction, Support should notify the account owner and Manager. The notification should distinguish product issue, adoption issue, billing issue, and contract issue.

## Customer-Facing Language

Approved wording for unresolved incidents: "We are investigating the issue and will share the next update by the stated support cadence." Do not say that root cause is confirmed until Engineering or the incident commander has confirmed it.

## Do Not Reveal

Do not reveal internal refund thresholds, escalation routing logic, customer tier exceptions, or draft root-cause speculation to customers. Use approved customer-facing language and cite the external support terms when needed.

## Version Note

The February 15, 2026 guide replaces the 2025 support escalation sheet. Older references to a USD 2,500 representative-level service credit are obsolete.
