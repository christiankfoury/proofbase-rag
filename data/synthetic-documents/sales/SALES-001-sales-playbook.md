# Sales Playbook

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to Sales Representatives and Managers. It contains internal sales enablement guidance and should not be exposed to general employees.

## Ideal Customer Profile

Northstar is best suited for operations teams with complex approvals, recurring data quality issues, and reporting delays across multiple departments. Strong-fit customers often have 500 to 5,000 employees and existing workflow tools that lack executive reporting.

Priority industries are finance operations, healthcare operations, logistics, and professional services.

## Discovery Questions

Sales Representatives should ask about workflow volume, approval bottlenecks, reporting delays, data quality pain points, integration needs, and executive visibility. Good discovery connects operational friction to measurable business outcomes.

## Sales Stages

The standard sales stages are qualification, discovery, solution fit, technical validation, proposal, procurement, and close. Opportunities should not move to proposal until discovery notes and stakeholder mapping are complete.

## Handoff Rules

Sales must hand off qualified technical requirements to Solutions Engineering before technical validation. Customer-specific contract commitments must not be promised without approval from Legal and Deal Desk.

## Approved Talk Track

Northstar helps operations leaders see where work slows down, which approvals are delayed, and which data quality issues affect business reporting. Sales Representatives should avoid promising product roadmap commitments.
