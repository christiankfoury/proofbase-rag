# Product Positioning and FAQ

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to Sales Representatives and Managers. It contains internal product positioning guidance.

## Core Value Proposition

Northstar Analytics gives operations teams one place to monitor workflows, approvals, data quality issues, and executive KPIs. The strongest message is operational visibility with measurable process improvement.

## Industry Use Cases

Finance operations teams use Northstar to track approval delays and reconciliation exceptions. Healthcare operations teams use it to monitor non-clinical workflow bottlenecks. Logistics teams use it to track exception queues and service-level risks. Professional services teams use it to monitor delivery workflows and client reporting readiness.

## Implementation Timeline

The typical implementation range is 6 to 10 weeks for standard deployments. Complex integrations or unusual data governance requirements may extend the timeline.

## Integrations

Northstar supports common data warehouse, ticketing, and workflow integrations through approved connectors and APIs. Sales Representatives should confirm exact integration support with Solutions Engineering.

## Product Limitations

Northstar is not a replacement for a system of record, identity provider, ERP, or clinical decision system. Sales Representatives should not promise unsupported integrations, exact roadmap delivery dates, or customer-specific contract commitments.
