# Manager Handbook

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to Managers. It contains manager-only operating guidance.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for setting clear expectations, maintaining team operating rhythm, supporting employee growth, approving time off, and escalating risks early. Managers should document important decisions and keep role expectations current.

## One-on-One Expectations

Managers should hold one-on-one meetings with direct reports at least every two weeks. Notes should focus on priorities, blockers, feedback, and growth needs. Sensitive personal details should not be recorded unless required for an HR process.

## Team Planning

Managers should maintain a quarterly team plan with goals, ownership, dependencies, and risks. Plans should identify customer-impacting work, hiring assumptions, and capacity constraints.

## Escalation Paths

Employee relations concerns should be escalated to People Operations. Security access issues should be escalated to IT Support. Customer-impacting delivery risks should be escalated to the relevant department leader.

## Conflict Handling

Managers should address team conflict early, document observed behavior, and avoid making unsupported conclusions about intent. Repeated conduct concerns should be discussed with People Operations.
