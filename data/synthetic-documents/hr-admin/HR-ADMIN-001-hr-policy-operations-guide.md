# HR Policy Operations Guide

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to HR Admin users. It contains internal HR operations guidance and should not be used as employee-facing policy language.

## Policy Ownership

People Operations owns the employee handbook, PTO and leave policy, benefits overview, and remote work policy. HR Admins are responsible for maintaining document versions, effective dates, and review cycles.

Policy updates require review by People Operations, Legal, and the executive owner when the update affects employee obligations, leave eligibility, benefits access, or employee relations workflows.

## Sensitive Case Handling

Sensitive employee relations cases must be logged in the HR case system. HR Admins should record the case category, intake source, assigned HR partner, and next review date.

Detailed HR investigation outcomes are not published in the general employee knowledge base and should not be summarized by the knowledge agent for non-HR users.

## Escalation Paths

Cases involving discrimination, harassment, retaliation, workplace safety, or executive-level conflicts should be escalated to the Director of People Operations within one business day.

Benefits eligibility disputes should be routed to the benefits specialist. Remote work exceptions involving cross-border work should be reviewed with People Operations and Legal before approval.

## Employee-Facing Guidance

HR Admins should keep employee-facing answers factual, policy-based, and free from legal conclusions. When a policy is unclear, the employee should be directed to People Operations instead of receiving speculative guidance.

## Policy Maintenance

Each HR policy should be reviewed at least annually. Changes must include a version number, effective date, owner, and summary of material changes.
