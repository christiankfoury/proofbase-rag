# Vendor, Travel, and Equipment Operations Policy

## Policy Scope

This policy covers vendor onboarding, business travel booking, equipment requests, shipping, and operations exceptions. It applies to employees, managers, HR Admins, and IT Admins who request operational support.

Finance controls reimbursement and procurement thresholds. Legal controls contract review. IT controls device security. This Operations policy explains process ownership and routing.

## Vendor Onboarding

New vendors require a vendor intake request before work begins. The requester must provide vendor legal name, business purpose, expected start date, estimated annual spend, data access level, and contract owner.

| Vendor Risk | Example | Required Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Office supplies, training materials | Operations |
| Moderate | Contractor with project files | Operations and Legal |
| High | Vendor processing company or customer data | Operations, Legal, IT Admin |
| Financial | Vendor paid through invoice or purchase order | Operations and Finance |

Vendors may not receive company data, customer data, credentials, or building access until required reviews are complete.

## Travel Booking

Employees should book business travel at least 14 days before departure when practical. Airfare, hotel, and ground transportation should be booked through the approved travel tool unless an emergency, accessibility need, or customer requirement makes that impractical.

Managers approve travel before booking when the trip is not already listed in the department plan. Travel booked after manager denial is not reimbursable unless the denial is reversed in writing.

## Equipment Requests

Standard equipment includes a laptop, charger, headset, keyboard, mouse, and monitor. New-hire equipment is initiated by HR Admin and fulfilled by IT Admin. Replacement equipment requires the employee to state whether the request is due to breakage, loss, upgrade, or accessibility need.

Equipment above the standard bundle requires manager approval. Lost equipment that may contain company data must be reported to IT Admin immediately and should not wait for the next business day.

## Shipping and Return Rules

Employees leaving the company must return company equipment within 10 business days unless HR Admin approves an extension. Operations may provide a prepaid shipping label. Employees should not include personal items in return shipments.

Damaged or missing equipment is reviewed by HR Admin and IT Admin before any payroll or final-pay action. This policy does not authorize payroll deductions by itself.

## Exceptions

Operations may approve exceptions for accessibility, business continuity, safety, or customer-critical travel. Repeated exceptions should be converted into a budget request, vendor onboarding request, or role-specific equipment standard.

## Overlap With Other Policies

Expense reimbursement follows Finance policy. Contract signature follows Legal policy. Device configuration and data security follow IT security policy. When policies overlap, the stricter approval path applies.
