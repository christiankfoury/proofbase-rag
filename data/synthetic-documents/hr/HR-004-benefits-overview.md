# Benefits Overview

## Health Benefits

Northstar provides health, dental, and vision benefits to eligible full-time employees. Coverage begins on the first day of the month after the employee's start date. Plan details vary by employee location.

This overview does not include provider contracts, claim adjudication rules, or legal advice about benefits eligibility.

## Wellness Stipend

Eligible full-time employees receive a wellness stipend of 750 CAD or 550 USD per calendar year, based on payroll country. The stipend may be used for fitness memberships, mental health apps, ergonomic equipment, or wellness programs.

Unused wellness stipend amounts do not carry over into the next calendar year.

## Learning Budget

Employees receive an annual learning budget of 1,500 CAD or 1,100 USD for approved courses, books, conferences, and certifications related to their role. Manager approval is required before purchase.

Learning budget requests should include the business purpose and expected outcome. The learning budget is separate from required compliance training.

## Retirement Matching

Northstar offers retirement contribution matching after 3 months of service. The company match is up to 4% of eligible earnings, subject to payroll country rules and plan eligibility.

Exact compensation formulas and salary bands are not included in this benefits overview.

## Benefits Support

Employees should contact the benefits support queue for enrollment questions, coverage questions, or life event changes. Urgent benefits questions should not be posted in public team channels.
