# PTO and Leave Policy

## Vacation Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation is accrued monthly and may be used after the employee's first 30 days of employment. Part-time employees receive prorated vacation based on scheduled weekly hours.

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they expire unless local law requires a different outcome.

## Vacation Approval Workflow

Vacation requests of 1 to 5 business days should be submitted at least 10 business days in advance. Requests longer than 5 business days should be submitted at least 20 business days in advance.

Managers approve vacation based on business coverage, project deadlines, and fairness across the team. Managers should not deny vacation without explaining the business reason.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 8 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick time may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or short-term caregiving needs. Employees should notify their manager as soon as practical when taking sick leave.

Medical details should not be shared in team channels. People Operations may request documentation only when required by policy or law.

## Company Holidays

Northstar observes 11 company holidays each year. Holiday calendars may vary by employee location. Employees should follow the holiday calendar assigned to their work location in the HR system.

## Parental Leave Summary

Eligible full-time employees may receive up to 12 weeks of company-supported parental leave after 6 months of service. Local statutory benefits may apply separately. Employees should contact People Operations at least 60 days before the expected leave date when possible.

This document provides a company policy summary and does not provide legal advice.

## Missing Policy Areas

Northstar does not publish a sabbatical policy in the employee knowledge base. Employees asking about sabbaticals should contact People Operations.
