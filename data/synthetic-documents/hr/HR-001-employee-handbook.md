# Employee Handbook

## Company Overview

Northstar Analytics builds analytics and workflow automation software for operations teams in finance, healthcare operations, logistics, and professional services. Employees are expected to protect customer trust, communicate clearly, and make decisions that improve operational reliability.

Northstar has offices in Toronto, Montreal, and New York. The company supports hybrid work and approved remote work across Canada and the United States.

## Company Values

Northstar's operating values are clarity, ownership, evidence, and trust. Employees should make decisions with evidence, document important work, and raise risks early. Teams are expected to treat internal policies as source-of-truth guidance rather than informal suggestions.

## Working Hours

Standard collaboration hours are 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local time from Monday to Thursday. Teams may define additional overlap windows when customer coverage or release coordination requires it.

Employees should keep calendars accurate and mark focus time when unavailable. Repeated inability to meet team collaboration expectations should be discussed with the employee's manager.

## Code of Conduct

Employees must communicate respectfully, protect confidential information, avoid harassment, and follow company security policies. Concerns about conduct should be reported to a manager, People Operations, or the confidential ethics channel.

The handbook does not include legal advice, salary bands, compensation formulas, or detailed HR investigation outcomes.

## Onboarding Basics

New employees complete onboarding during their first 10 business days. Required onboarding includes security training, benefits enrollment, department orientation, and access review. Managers are responsible for assigning a buddy and scheduling the first 30-day check-in.

Employees should complete mandatory security awareness training before accessing customer data or production systems.

## Employee Support Channels

General HR questions should be sent to People Operations. Benefits questions should be sent to the benefits support queue. Security access questions should be sent to IT Support. Performance or role expectation questions should first be discussed with the employee's manager.

## Related Policies

For time off, consult the PTO and Leave Policy. For remote work, consult the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy. For acceptable technology use, consult the Acceptable Use Policy.
