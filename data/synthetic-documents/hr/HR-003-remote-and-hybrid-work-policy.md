# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Work Model

Northstar supports hybrid work and approved remote work. Employees assigned to Toronto, Montreal, or New York offices are expected to attend office collaboration days when scheduled by their department.

Remote employees may work from approved locations in Canada or the United States if their role, tax location, and security requirements support remote work.

## Approval Requirements

Employees must receive manager approval before changing their regular work location. A temporary change within the same country for fewer than 15 business days may be approved by the manager. Longer changes require People Operations review.

Managers should consider team coverage, customer needs, time zone overlap, and data handling requirements before approving remote work.

## Cross-Border Work

Northstar does not guarantee approval for work from any country. Temporary cross-border work requires People Operations review before travel. The company does not maintain a formal visa or immigration policy in the employee knowledge base.

Requests to work outside Canada or the United States are considered ambiguous until People Operations reviews tax, employment, and security implications.

## Office Expectations

Hybrid teams should define collaboration days at least one month in advance. Employees should attend required team planning, onboarding, incident review, and customer workshop sessions in person when asked by their manager.

## Security Expectations

Remote work must follow the Device and BYOD Security Policy and the Data Classification and Handling Policy. Confidential and restricted data may only be accessed on approved devices using company-approved tools.

## Not Covered

This policy does not provide legal advice, immigration advice, or guaranteed approval for international remote work.
