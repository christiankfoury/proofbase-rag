# Device and BYOD Security Policy

## Company Devices

Employees who regularly access company systems should use a company-managed device. Company devices must have disk encryption, endpoint protection, automatic screen lock, and current operating system updates.

Employees may not disable security tools or remove device management controls from company devices.

## Personal Devices

Personal devices may be used for limited work access only when enrolled in approved mobile device management or when access occurs through approved browser-based tools. Personal devices must use a passcode, supported operating system, and automatic lock.

Restricted data must not be downloaded to personal devices. Confidential data should be accessed only through approved company systems.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

Employees must report lost or stolen devices to IT Support within 2 hours of discovery. The report should include the device type, last known location, whether customer data may have been accessed, and whether the device was company-managed or personal.

## Local Storage

Customer data, restricted data, and regulated data should not be stored locally unless there is a documented business need and approved protection. Files should be stored in company-approved cloud storage with access controls.

## Remote Work

Remote work must follow this policy and the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy. Employees working remotely are responsible for using secure networks, avoiding shared public computers, and protecting screens from unauthorized viewing.
