# Expense, Procurement, and Reimbursement Policy

## Policy Status

This policy is effective February 1, 2026 and replaces the 2025 informal spend guidance. If an older team wiki or email thread lists a different limit, this policy controls unless Finance publishes a newer version.

Employees should submit expenses within 30 calendar days of the transaction date. Expenses submitted after 60 calendar days require manager approval and a Finance Operations exception note.

## Expense Categories

| Category | Standard Limit | Approval Required |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Meals during business travel | USD 85 per day | Manager approval above limit |
| Customer-facing meals | USD 125 per attendee | Manager approval and attendee list |
| Office supplies | USD 300 per purchase | Manager approval above limit |
| Software subscription trial | USD 500 annualized value | IT review and manager approval |
| Conference registration | USD 1,500 per event | Manager approval before purchase |

Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of a customer-facing meal approved by a manager before the event. Gift cards, personal devices, fines, commuting costs, and family travel are not reimbursable.

## Receipt Requirements

Receipts are required for every expense above USD 25. A credit card statement is not enough by itself when the merchant receipt is available. Missing receipts require a signed missing-receipt attestation that explains the business purpose, amount, date, and merchant.

Expense reports must include the business purpose in plain language. A report labeled only "meeting," "travel," or "miscellaneous" will be returned for correction.

## Procurement Thresholds

Purchases below USD 2,500 may be approved by the employee's manager if the purchase is within the team's approved budget. Purchases from USD 2,500 to USD 10,000 require Finance review before a purchase order is issued.

Purchases above USD 10,000 require Finance review, Legal review if a contract is involved, and department leader approval. Vendors that will process company data must complete the vendor security review before contract signature, even when the spend is below USD 10,000.

## Reimbursement Timing

Approved expense reports are reimbursed in the next regular payroll cycle when submitted at least five business days before payroll close. Reports submitted after the cutoff move to the following payroll cycle.

Finance may hold reimbursement if the report lacks required receipts, approval, business purpose, or attendee information. The hold is administrative and does not imply misconduct.

## Exceptions

Emergency expenses are allowed when waiting for approval would create a customer, employee safety, or business-continuity risk. The employee must document the emergency and obtain manager approval within three business days.

Recurring exceptions must be converted into a purchase order or department budget request. Finance does not approve repeated one-off exceptions for predictable monthly costs.

## Related Policies

Travel bookings must follow the Operations travel process. Vendor contracts must follow Legal approval rules. Software purchases must follow IT security review rules before reimbursement or procurement approval.
