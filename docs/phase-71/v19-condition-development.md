General hypothesis: coverage needs to distinguish a condition communicated by a
direct contextual answer from a merely sufficient example or a waived requirement.
Do not implement a blanket after/only-after equivalence. Preserve every actor,
action, object, quantity and material condition. A direct answer about when an
action is permitted can communicate its matching prerequisite without repeating
"only"; an explicitly optional, bypassed, narrowed or incomplete condition cannot.
Question context resolves the requested relation, never supplies missing policy
facts. Keep factual and citation entailment separate from coverage.
