## Decision

Recommend a separately scoped **offline checker-contract and condition-preservation
work unit**, before buying more observations or changing retrieval/model architecture.
Its concrete starting evidence is V4 tasks01/09 (condition errors accepted) and
task12 (a supported answer core withheld after malformed checker bindings). Preserve
all seven checker criteria, exact citations and fail-safe delivery. First expose
the specific failed unit/binding in diagnostic output and test a structured binding
design against these saved cases plus the successful quotation, correction, no-source,
conflict and permission controls. Do not auto-accept malformed output or claim
offline tests prove the next model will preserve conditions. This report does not
implement or authorize that follow-up.

Ranked alternatives:

1. **Checker contract and condition preservation:** direct captured application
   evidence and an offline reproduction exist. Require a broader saved-case matrix
   and a new scoped implementation instruction; any subsequent model validation
   needs separate funding/scope authorization.
2. **Evaluator correspondence design:** required before release qualification.
   ER2 deliberately leaves 31/44 pairs unresolved. Exact free-form meaning equality
   cannot establish semantic equivalence generally. A structured semantic binding
   design or an explicitly approved adjudication policy must preserve all genuine
   disagreements and uncertainty; neither is adopted here. Task02's required-fact
   relevance also remains unresolved, with its original failure intact.
3. **Normal-retrieval acquisition:** useful to establish the missing observation,
   but current evidence does not implicate ranking. The bounded proposal below
   tests tiny controlled corpora, not broad enterprise retrieval quality. Do not
   buy it as a presumed retrieval fix or as a route to V6 activation.

No model/provider switch, extra agents, reranker, fine-tuning, fresh holdout,
automatic correction cycle or release run follows this report. V4 stays active;
V6 stays experimental, disabled and unaccepted. Phase73 remains closed.

## Prospective acquisition proposal — not authorized or recommended to launch now

If normal-retrieval observations are requested later, use the manifest's frozen
12 V4 turn contexts and histories, two repetitions of each arm: 48 application
requests. Alternate arm order per repetition. The reference arm supplies the exact
selected passages; the normal arm searches an isolated, immutable per-case index
of the declared source IDs with role filtering, top-k5, vector/lexical reranking,
multi-doc off. Seven unique source chunks require at most seven embedding calls;
24 normal-arm queries reserve one embedding each. Corpus setup is local and
deterministic; no AI cleanup or additional source rewriting. The private source
remains indexed for its exclusion control. Tiny corpora limit retrieval inference.

Every application stage reserves 16,384 input tokens and 2,048 output tokens.
The one existing repair allowance includes a second generator and checker call;
provider retries remain zero. Embeddings reserve 8,192 input tokens per call.
Unexpected queries/calls or larger payloads stop before submission; no truncation,
reduced output allowances or automatic cap increase. No live executor exists here.
