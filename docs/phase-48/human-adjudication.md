This is materially less evidence than the completed Phase 47 adjudication had. The labels below therefore distinguish what the retained metadata proves from what the interruption makes unknowable; they do not reconstruct missing answers.

## Classification Rules

- `evaluator-only`: retained evidence establishes that the user-visible result is substantively correct and safe, while a deterministic scoring limitation alone caused the machine failure.
- `product`: retained evidence independently establishes a behavior, retrieval, memory-resolution, or answer-coverage gap. The label does not assert anything about missing answer text.
- `mixed`: retained evidence establishes both a product gap and a materially misleading evaluator diagnosis.
- `indeterminate`: answer text or citation payloads lost in the interruption are necessary to distinguish a real product gap from an evaluator-only failure.

No case is labeled `mixed`: the retained evidence can prove several product gaps, but it cannot prove that any additional lost diagnostic was also misleading.

The only defensible statements are the observed counts and costs above, the per-case metadata findings, the evaluator-only adjudication of `P48-H2-UP-001`, and the explicit uncertainty for evidence lost to interruption.
