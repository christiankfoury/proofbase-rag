in the grader and review requests; these are not missing-metadata transport errors.
They concern consistent application of existing meaning, not permission to
relax standards. All original model outputs, disputed judgments and references
are retained. No inspection-derived replacement labels or adjusted score exist.

Four invalid grading rows also receive no credit: 004 has non-exact coverage
spans; 009, 011 and 018 exhaust their 4,096 output-token limits in reasoning and
return empty content. There were no retries. Other reviewer-caught disagreements
remain unresolved even when inspection agrees with the reviewer's reasoning.
The complete per-case evidence is in [the case inventory](v6-case-review.md).

All 60 cases have saved application responses and completed evaluation rows;
completion does not imply every model judgment is valid. Role/project checks and
source inspection observed no unauthorized retrieval or disclosure. Department
scope is null throughout: this suite does not establish department-only isolation,
production security assurance or independent human adjudication. Original v4/v5
failed evidence and the unknown v4 call's full reservation remain unchanged.

## Cause-driven follow-up boundary

General evaluator hypotheses are inconsistent contextual interpretation and
speech-act classification, plus structurally invalid output under fixed bounds.
Separately confirmed application concerns include numeric validation of scenario
values/thousands-separated amounts, unnecessary abstention, mixed-request coverage,
modality strengthening and unsupported extra rationale. These are diagnostic
observations, not a controlled improvement result. Any repair must use separately
composed development variants with negative controls, unchanged scoring meaning,
full qualification, another freeze and newly isolated cases. This exposed suite
cannot be resumed, selectively regraded, relabeled or reused as a fresh holdout.
